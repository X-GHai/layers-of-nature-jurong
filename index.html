04 · PERIOD IMAGES

What did the landscape look like in the selected period?

The cards update with the selected timeline year. Official records are supplemented by community photographs and videos when the post, upload date or capture date can be stated. A card is labelled exactly dated only when the source dates the image to that state; otherwise it is shown as a nearest dated reference. Community previews remain linked to the original post and retain the creator’s copyright.

05 · FACILITIES AND AMENITIES

Named facilities, mapped with stated limits.

The register follows the NParks Jurong Lake Gardens brochure and turns named attractions, amenity nodes and access points into clickable map objects. These are plan-derived orientation points, not authoritative survey coordinates. A point hidden before 2024 marks an evidence boundary: it does not mean the site was empty or unused.

06 · METHOD AND SOURCES

Every mapped object has a source status.

The map is deliberately conservative. A source can document a feature without giving us a survey-grade polygon. Those cases are either shown as audited point anchors with no boundary claim, or retained as source-only narratives when even a defensible anchor cannot be identified.
